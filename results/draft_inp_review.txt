REVIEW OF Tunduma_Transmission_Main_DRAFT.inp
====================================================================================================
 1. Reservoir head is the ground level, not the river water level
    N0001 head = 1365.94 m (= ground at the intake). River Momba survey points are 1360.7-1364.5 m; model uses LWL 1361.0 m / HWL 1364.5 m.
    With the head at ground level the intake suction and low-lift pump head are wrong.

 2. No pumps in the model
    ER stations: MOMBA_INTAKE Q=978 m3/h H=10 m (1W+1S); GRIT_TANK Q=978 m3/h H=25 m (1W+1S); MOMBA_WTP Q=304 m3/h H=330 m (3W+1S); NKANGAMO_BPS Q=455 m3/h H=221 m (2W+1S)
    Without pumps a reservoir at ~1366 m cannot supply tanks at ~1700 m - EPANET gives large negative pressures and flow running back from the tanks to the river.

 3. Tank N2163 has placeholder geometry
    D = 15 m, levels 0-6 m -> 1,060 m3; elevation 1699.34 m. Replace with the real tank floor, BWL/TWL and diameter (see tunduma_config.json).

 4. Tank N2250 has placeholder geometry
    D = 15 m, levels 0-6 m -> 1,060 m3; elevation 1684.57 m. Replace with the real tank floor, BWL/TWL and diameter (see tunduma_config.json).

 5. Tank N2338 has placeholder geometry
    D = 15 m, levels 0-6 m -> 1,060 m3; elevation 1523.81 m. Replace with the real tank floor, BWL/TWL and diameter (see tunduma_config.json).

 6. N2163 is modelled as a tank but is junction J-003N
    ER schematic: J-003N is a pipe junction on the BPS rising main (Ipito offtake); Uhuru Park GBR sits just after it and the Chapwa/Makambini line leaves the GBR by gravity.

 7. GRIT_TANK missing from the model
    Grit tank - high lift PS (elevation 1365.94 m): off the surveyed route, fed by branch LL_DELIVERY from MOMBA_INTAKE (ASSUMED short delivery pipe low-lift PS -> grit tank) - not in the draft.

 8. MOMBA_WTP missing from the model
    Momba WTP (20,000 m3/day) (elevation 1372.1 m): at alignment node N0021 - the draft runs the pipe straight through it as a junction.

 9. IKANA_GBR missing from the model
    Ikana (Momba) GBR 3,000 m3 (elevation 1663.0 m): at alignment node N0412 - the draft runs the pipe straight through it as a junction.

10. NKANGAMO_BPS missing from the model
    Nkangamo BPS (elevation 1555.5 m): sits inside the inferred pipe GAP-BPS-approach - the draft has no station there.

11. TUNDUMA_ESR missing from the model
    Existing Tunduma ESR 120 m3 (elevation 1700.0 m): off the surveyed route, fed by branch ESR_BRANCH from J-001N (STEEL DN100-PN10, L = 38 m (schematic label DN80)) - not in the draft.

12. UHURU_GSR01 missing from the model
    Existing Tunduma Uhuru GSR 01 500 m3 (elevation 1698.4 m): off the surveyed route, fed by branch GSR01_BRANCH from J-002N (STEEL DN150-PN16, L = 40 m) - not in the draft.

13. MAKAMBINI_GSR03 missing from the model
    Existing Tunduma Makambini GSR 03 (elevation 1655.7 m): off the surveyed route, fed by branch MAKAMBINI_BRANCH from J-004N (STEEL DN100-PN10, L = 494 m (route not surveyed - straight line)) - not in the draft.
